# Sabha AI: Hackathon Demonstration Guide

This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough to demonstrate the core capabilities of Sabha AI to hackathon judges, technical evaluators, and new team members.

---

## 🎬 3-Minute Presentation Script

### Step 1: Open Sabha AI
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Notice the sleek, enterprise dark navy design system, Sanskrit council motif, and the **Qualcomm Cloud AI** telemetry badge in the top right.
3. Observe the product tagline: **"Every conversation. A smarter decision."**

---

### Step 2: Demonstrate Dual-Input Mode

#### Option 1: Mode B — Transcript Lab (Recommended for Fast Evaluation)
1. Click **"Transcript Lab"** in the top navigation or sidebar.
2. Notice the three preloaded demonstration scenarios at the top right:
   - **Launch (Sample A)**: Product launch readiness & scope lock.
   - **At Risk (Sample B)**: Database migration risk & cross-team blockers.
   - **Creative (Sample C)**: Infrastructure cost overrun & alternative architectures.
3. Click **"Launch" (Sample A)**:
   - The editor populates with realistic meeting dialog.
   - Observe the live telemetry bar: Word count, Segment count, Estimated Tokens, and Estimated Cost calculated instantly.
4. Click **"Analyze Transcript"**:
   - The Context Sieve compresses the transcript while preserving negations and commitments.
   - The Model Router routes tasks to Qualcomm models.
   - The system transitions directly into the **Unified Results Dashboard**.

#### Option 2: Mode A — Live Meeting
1. Click **"Meetings (Live)"** $\rightarrow$ **"New Live Meeting"**.
2. Enter a title (e.g., *"Sprint 24 Architectural Sync"*) and click **"Launch Room"**.
3. You are now inside the LiveKit WebRTC video stage:
   - Toggle Microphone and Camera controls.
   - Type in the speech simulation bar on the right to stream real-time transcript segments.
   - When finished, click **"End & Analyze (Sabha Intelligence)"**.

---

### Step 3: Walk Through the Flagship Results Dashboard

Once the analysis completes, highlight these five core sections:

#### 1. Executive Summary & Telemetry Header
- Show the **Model Attribution**: `Qualcomm Llama-3.1-8B`
- Show the **Compression Ratio**: Notice that Context Sieve compressed the input by **~40%**, saving significant LLM costs.
- Show the **Estimated Cost**: Completely transparent cost accounting.

#### 2. Flagship Second Brain Review Tab
Click on **"Second Brain Review"**:
- **A. What We Believe**:
  - Show the confirmed launch date (*"Web and Android on November 15th"*).
  - Show Vikram's commitment to payment gateway load testing.
  - Point out that every single item includes a verbatim evidence quote.
- **B. What We Might Be Missing**:
  - Show the flagged blind spot: *"EU GDPR compliance sign-off lacks ownership and legal SLA"*.
  - Show the explanation of why launching without GDPR exposes the company to regulatory fines.
- **C. What to Investigate Next**:
  - Show recommended follow-ups (e.g., 3D Secure ACS load testing).
  - Click **"+ Convert to Task"** to turn the AI recommendation into a tracked team action item.

#### 3. Action Items & Evidence Validation Tab
Click on **"Decisions & Action Items"**:
- Point out Vikram's task: Owner is explicitly confirmed because he said *"I will handle..."*.
- Point out the EU GDPR task: **Owner is Unassigned**. Explain that Sabha AI **never invents an owner** when someone asks *"Who is handling...?"*.
- Point out the one-click **"Confirm"** and **"Dispute"** buttons on decisions.

#### 4. Creative Solutions Engine Tab
Click on **"Creative Solutions Engine"** (or load **Sample C**):
- Show the identified bottleneck: *"Surging audio transcription compute costs ($18k/mo vs $5k cap)"*.
- Show the 3 distinct architectural alternatives generated:
  1. *Client-side WASM VAD pre-filtering*.
  2. *Qualcomm Cloud AI dedicated hardware inference*.
  3. *Adaptive audio sample rate downsampling*.
- Show the trade-off matrix: Expected benefits, limitations, required resources, and evaluation criteria scores.

#### 5. Research Assistant Tab
Click on **"Research Assistant"**:
- Type a technical question, e.g.: *"What are the benchmark throughput gains of Qualcomm Cloud AI hardware accelerators?"*
- Click **"Run Research"**.
- The system fetches verified external sources and synthesizes a research brief using Qualcomm Cloud AI, with **verifiable clickable citations**.

---

### Step 4: Show Manager & Team Dashboard
1. Click **"Team Workspace"** in the sidebar.
2. Point out that all metrics are **deterministic SQL calculations**:
   - Total Action Items, Completion Rate, and Unassigned counts.
   - Workload matrix grouped by participant.
   - Urgent Project Blockers board highlighting blocked dependencies.
3. Emphasize that Sabha AI **never calls an LLM to do basic arithmetic or database queries**.

---

### Step 5: Exporting Reports
1. Click **"Insights & Reports"** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Markdown (.md)** or **Spreadsheet (.csv)**.
3. Click **"Export"** to download the complete report with evidence references.
